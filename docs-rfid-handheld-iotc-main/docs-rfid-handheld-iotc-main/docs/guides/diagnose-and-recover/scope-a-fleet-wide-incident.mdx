The other diagnosis pages assume one reader. When several readers fail at once, first find out how far the problem reaches and what the affected readers have in common. The answer decides where the cause is: in the readers, or in something they share.

## Step 1: Watch the Whole Tenant {#watch}

The reader's serial number is always the last topic segment, so a `+` in that position subscribes to one kind of message from every reader in a tenant. With the conventional middle segments:

```text
acme/MGMT/clients/rfid/+
acme/MGMT/clients/event/+
```

The first shows `mqttConnEVT` and the Last Will from every reader; the second shows `heartBeatEVT` and `alerts`. Take each message's reader from its topic. Use your endpoints' actual middle segments.

## Step 2: Count the Affected Readers {#count}

For each reader, record the latest evidence and its age: the last `CONNECTED` or `DISCONNECTED`, the last `heartBeatEVT`, the last alert. A reader with no recent heartbeat is missing evidence, not proven down; heartbeats travel at QoS 0. See [Build a Fleet Health View](/guides/operate-and-monitor/build-a-fleet-health-view).

## Step 3: Find What They Share {#correlate}

Compare the affected readers with the healthy ones:

| Shared factor | How to see it | Points at |
|---|---|---|
| The same site, access point or network | Your inventory records, `get_wifi` | The network |
| The same broker or endpoint configuration | `get_endpoint_config` | The broker or its access rules |
| The same firmware version | `get_version` | A firmware change |
| A recent change applied to them | Your change and rollout records | That change |
| The same alert | `alerts` `id` | The condition the alert names |

Read the readers you can still reach, and pace the commands: each reader queues at most 10.

## Step 4: Decide Where the Cause Is {#decide}

- **Every reader on one broker fails together:** check the broker, its certificates and its access rules before any reader. See [MQTT Connection](/guides/diagnose-and-recover/diagnose-by-system-area/mqtt-connection).
- **Every reader at one site fails:** check the network there. See [Network and Connectivity](/guides/diagnose-and-recover/diagnose-by-system-area/network-and-connectivity).
- **The readers that took a change fail:** stop the rollout. See [Know Your Rollback Triggers](/guides/scale-to-a-fleet/roll-out-changes-in-stages#3-know-your-rollback-triggers).
- **No common factor:** treat each reader as its own case, starting from [Diagnose and Recover](/guides/diagnose-and-recover).

## Step 5: Record and Escalate {#record}

Keep the list of affected readers, the shared factor, the times and the evidence. If you need Zebra, include them with the evidence in [Escalate to Support](/guides/diagnose-and-recover/escalate-to-support).

Some failures leave no MQTT path to fix them: every management endpoint points at an unreachable broker, a TLS change failed on the only endpoint, or the reader needs a different regulatory region, which no MQTT command can change. Recovery then starts again from bootstrap.

## When You Need This {#when}

- No MQTT command reaches the reader, and [RP-02](/guides/diagnose-and-recover/recovery-playbooks/restore-mqtt-connectivity#rp-02) could not restore an endpoint.
- The reader must operate in a different regulatory region.
- The reader is being moved to another deployment, or retired.

## Before You Begin {#before-you-begin}

- Keep the reader's baseline: the configuration it should have, per domain. See [Keep Configuration Consistent](/guides/scale-to-a-fleet/keep-configuration-consistent#baseline).
- Have 123RFID Desktop, a USB-C cable and a Windows computer.

## Step 1: Reset the Reader, If Needed {#reset}

The MQTT API has no factory-reset command, and the available sources do not describe how to factory-reset a reader or what a reset clears. Follow the reader's product guide, or ask Zebra support. Often a reset is not needed: 123RFID Desktop can correct the endpoint and Wi-Fi settings of a reader it can connect to.

## Step 2: Bootstrap Again {#bootstrap}

Connect the reader to 123RFID Desktop over USB-C, and set its region, Wi-Fi profile and first MQTT endpoint, as in [Bootstrap a Reader with 123RFID Desktop](/guides/manage-your-reader/bootstrap-reader). Confirm over MQTT with [`get_version`](/api-reference/mqtt/mgmt/get-version).

## Step 3: Re-Apply the Baseline {#re-apply}

Apply the reader's baseline domain by domain, in the order in [Provision Readers](/guides/scale-to-a-fleet/provision-readers#apply-baseline): certificates, Wi-Fi, endpoints, event reporting (then a restart), operating mode and post-filters. Verify each domain by reading it back.

**Success check:** the reader matches its baseline in every domain you can read, and its heartbeats and alerts arrive.

## Retiring a Reader {#retire}

After you reset a reader that leaves your deployment, remove its broker credentials and access rules, so that its serial number cannot connect again.

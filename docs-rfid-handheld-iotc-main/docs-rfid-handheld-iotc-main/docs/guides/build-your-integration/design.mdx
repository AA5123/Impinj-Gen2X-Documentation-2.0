Two decisions come before any code: how readers reach your applications, and where the reader should publish tag data.

- [Plan Your Integration](/guides/build-your-integration/plan-your-integration): direct, bridge and gateway patterns, and what the broker side needs.
- [Choose Shared or Dedicated Data Endpoints](/guides/build-your-integration/choose-shared-or-dedicated-data-endpoints): tag data on a management endpoint, or on its own `DATA1` endpoint.

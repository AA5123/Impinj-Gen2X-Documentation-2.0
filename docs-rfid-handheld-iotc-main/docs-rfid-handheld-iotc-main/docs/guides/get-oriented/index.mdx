This documentation covers the **Zebra IoT Connector for handheld RFID**: the firmware on an RFD40 or RFD90 reader that lets your applications control it and receive its data over MQTT 3.1.1. The pages in this section help you decide whether it fits your job and what you need before you start.

- [What the IoT Connector Is](/guides/get-oriented/what-the-iot-connector-is): what it does, and what it does not.
- [What You Need](/guides/get-oriented/what-you-need): the reader, the broker, the network and the tools.

## Scope {#scope}

| Covered | Not covered |
|---|---|
| RFD40 Standard, RFD40 Premium, RFD40 Premium Plus, RFD40-M Premium Plus, RFD9030 and RFD9090 readers | Fixed readers, which are a different product line |
| The MQTT API: its 20 commands and 4 events |  |
| Bootstrap with 123RFID Desktop over USB-C | Other provisioning tools, except where noted |

## About the Content-Type Badges {#badges}

Every page carries one of four badges, following the [Diátaxis framework](https://diataxis.fr/):

- 📗 **Tutorial**: a guided lesson with a visible result. Read it to learn by doing.
- 📙 **How-to**: directions for a specific task. Read it to act.
- 📘 **Explanation**: what something is and why it works as it does. Read it to understand.
- 📕 **Reference**: exact facts about fields, values and codes. Look them up while you work.

## Learn by Doing {#tutorials}

Two tutorials take you from nothing to a working result:

- 📗 **[Quick Start: Read Your First Tag](/guides/quick-start)**: bootstrap a reader, send your first command, add endpoints and watch tag reads arrive.
- 📗 **[Build a Minimal Integration in Python](/guides/build-your-integration/build-a-minimal-integration)**: a short script that starts an inventory and collects unique tags.

After them, switch to the how-to guides for specific tasks, the explanations to understand why, and the reference to look things up.

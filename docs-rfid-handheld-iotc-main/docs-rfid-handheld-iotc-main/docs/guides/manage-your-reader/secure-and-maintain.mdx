These pages protect a reader's connection and keep its firmware current. Three of the commands involved finish after they reply: `install_certificate` and `set_os` report their outcome later in an `alerts` event, and `reboot` reports none. Subscribe to the event topic before you send them.

- [Install and Manage Certificates](/guides/manage-your-reader/install-and-manage-certificates): install, list, delete and rotate certificates.
- [Secure an Endpoint with TLS and Client Certificates](/guides/manage-your-reader/secure-an-endpoint): move an endpoint to TLS and choose the verification level.
- [Update Firmware and Restart the Reader](/guides/manage-your-reader/update-firmware-and-restart): `set_os` and `reboot`.

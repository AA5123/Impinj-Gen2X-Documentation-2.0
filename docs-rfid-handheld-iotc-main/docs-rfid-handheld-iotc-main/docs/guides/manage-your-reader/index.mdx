These pages cover one reader at a time: getting it onto the network and the broker, checking what it is, configuring its connections, securing them, and keeping its firmware current. To do the same across many readers, see [Scale to a Fleet](/guides/scale-to-a-fleet).

A reader's first connection is made outside MQTT, with 123RFID Desktop over USB-C. After that, everything on these pages is done with MQTT commands sent to the reader's management endpoint:

1. [Bootstrap a Reader with 123RFID Desktop](/guides/manage-your-reader/bootstrap-reader) gives the reader its region, Wi-Fi and first MQTT endpoint.
2. [Inspect Reader Identity and Status](/guides/manage-your-reader/inspect-reader-identity-and-status) confirms which reader you reached and what state it is in.
3. [Configure MQTT Endpoints](/guides/manage-your-reader/configure-mqtt-endpoints) and [Configure Wi-Fi](/guides/manage-your-reader/configure-wi-fi) shape its connections.
4. [Secure an Endpoint with TLS and Client Certificates](/guides/manage-your-reader/secure-an-endpoint) moves its traffic to TLS.

How configuration behaves across these domains is explained in [The Configuration Model](/guides/core-concepts/the-configuration-model).
